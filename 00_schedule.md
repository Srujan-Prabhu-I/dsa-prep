# The Schedule: 19 Oct 2026 → 20 Jun 2027

> **Deadline:** 4th year starts **21 Jun 2027**, and placement OAs follow soon after. The target is full readiness (curriculum §1 benchmarks) by **20 Jun 2027**.
> **Load:** ≈ 2–2.5 h/day on 6 days + the weekly contest. That's ≈ 15 h/week, ≈ 500 h in total, and it leaves the rest of your time for backend work.
> **Order of sacrifice if you fall behind:** stretch problems → Ch 37 → Ch 36 → Ch 25 → Ch 33 → Ch 35. Never cut re-solves, Mixed Sets, or Ch 1, 2, 27.

## 3. Adjusting the plan

* **Exam dates:** move the two FLEX weeks onto your actual end-semester exam dates. Every other week shifts by one.
* **Summer internship (May–Jul 2027):** if you have one, Phase 6 will run at half speed. In that case, start Ch 34 before Mixed Set VI and drop Ch 36–37.
* **Behind by more than 2 weeks at a checkpoint:** switch the remaining chapters to practice-only (no stretch). Don't skip Mixed Sets to catch up; they *are* the training for technique selection.
* **Ahead:** do the stretch problems of your two weakest chapters (by failure tag in the log). Don't jump ahead to new chapters.
